# Reviewing Phase Deliverables

Research, plan, low-fidelity design, and high-fidelity design each produce outputs that later phases rely on. Their review happens before that reliance, not after implementation has made the choice costly to reverse.

**Guidelines:**

- MUST apply the project's conduct practice for reviewing deliverables before accepting an investigation conclusion for planning, presenting a plan for approval, presenting each visual-option round for selection, or passing the selected output into dependent investigation or implementation.
- MUST bind the result to the actual artifact revision and required material under the execution handoff contracts. A materially changed artifact needs a current review within its remaining round budget; a review still covering the identical artifact, criteria, and necessary evidence is reusable, not repeated because its phase name changed.
- MUST carry the conduct practice's review identity, consumed rounds, and latest reviewed revision in the loop's existing recovery state; classify incomplete evidence through the execution handoff contracts rather than replaying the initial checkpoint.
- MUST use the conduct practice's completion boundary to select the next phase: a fully reviewed current revision with no unresolved findings, including at the third round, proceeds to the applicable human-approval or dependent-work gate; unavailable review or exhausted rounds with incomplete review or unresolved findings stops at a disclosed non-complete human handoff. Preserve plan revision identity and the existing low-fidelity then high-fidelity human-selection order; review is not approval.
- MUST NOT count these artifact reviews as implementation pre-flight or mandatory independent code review, or use those later stages to extend this artifact-review budget.

When the conduct capability is absent, use this standalone fallback for substantive outputs, not ordinary review findings, suggested corrections, responses, or purely administrative review coordination: review the actual investigation, plan, or design output against the original task and necessary evidence with a different author-independent reviewer in a fresh context, using a separate subagent by default or an equivalent context selected by applicable host guidance, without author receipts or past findings. Evaluate findings, improve the output, and re-review within at most three rounds, without extension. Consume a round before every new assessment request, failures and replacement assessments included; collecting the same pending result does not consume another. Preserve counts on recovery and stop when the remaining budget is unverified. A complete current-revision review with no unresolved findings may finish even in round three; otherwise, unavailable review or exhausted rounds requires a non-complete human handoff disclosing remaining findings and unreviewed corrections, not dependent execution. Never infer human approval. The fallback applies only for the absent owner.
